# Farm Idle

**Title:** Farm Idle
**Genre:** Idle + Match
**Tone:** Slightly comedic
**Reference:** Cow Evolution


## 1. Player Goal

Have the highest number of unlocked farms.

## 2. Core Loop

1. Animals poop, and the poop generates money.
2. The player matches animals, creating/discovering more and more mutant animals.
3. The rarer the animal, the more profitable its poop.
4. With the money, the player buys upgrades and/or new farms.
5. New farms lead to the discovery of new animals.

## 3. Features

The idea is to have several idle game features.

**Not yet implemented:**
- **Offline system per farm:** the farm generates money for X hours offline (very common in idle games).

## 4. Monetization

**Implemented:**
- **Pigeon:** generates a poop rain, meaning money (very common in idle games).
- **Tornado:** helps the player match animals when there are too many of them.

**Ideas:**
- Level 2 animals being generated every X minutes.
- Selling more hours of offline generation (if the game generates offline revenue for 2 hours, sell 4 hours — idle games have this).
- Revenue multiplier on poop: poop generates 2x the value it already generates.
- Ad break.
- Double offline revenue.
